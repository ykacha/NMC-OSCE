Escalation thresholds

1–4 Low: Reassess every 4–6 hours.
5–6 Medium or any single 3: Urgent review within 1 hour.
≥7 High: Emergency — escalate immediately.
New confusion (C/V/P/U): Always 3 — urgent regardless of total.

Drug Calculation Practice

Practice until automatic. No calculator needed in the OSCE.

Key formulas

Tablets: Prescribed ÷ Stock dose
Liquid: (Prescribed ÷ Stock) × Volume of stock
IV rate: Volume (mL) ÷ Time (hours) = mL/hr
Drops/min: (Volume × Drop factor) ÷ Time (mins)

Unit conversions

  • 1 g = 1000 mg
  • 1 mg = 1000 micrograms
  • 1 L = 1000 mL
  • Always convert to SAME unit first
  • Insulin: units are units — never convert

🚨 Instant fail — serious safety errors

Any ONE = automatic fail. Practice until these never happen.
  • Touch patient/equipment before hand hygiene
  • No patient identity check (name + DOB)
  • No consent or explanation given
  • Missed allergy check in medication/wound station
  • Medication error: wrong patient/dose/route/time/expiry
  • ANTT contamination of key site or sterile field
  • Unsafe sharps handling
  • Failure to escalate deterioration or abnormal NEWS2
  • Breach of confidentiality or unprofessional conduct
